# Notes

The TikTok strategy for TUT!/Tracey is not traditional app marketing.

Instead of polished ads, the TikToks are short "leaked moments" from Tracey's chaotic life — realistic screen recordings of explosive exchanges, custody selfies, voice notes, Facebook screenshots, Dave interruptions, and public embarrassment.

The clips should feel:

* real
* intrusive
* relatable
* socially believable

Not:

* heavily edited
* corporate
* "download my app" ads

Each TikTok acts like a tiny standalone social disaster:

* setup
* escalation
* reveal
* abrupt ending

Examples:

* "my phones in evidence"
* Dave interrupting with "have u heard 😭"
* Tracey accidentally going viral on fake TikTok inside the story universe
* Mum posting embarrassing Facebook statuses
* custody-area selfies
* "WHY'S IT THANKING ME IF IM STEALING"

The app itself provides the full ongoing relationship and continuity.
TikTok only shows the biggest moments.

The best-performing clips will likely:

* feel like accidental screen recordings
* contain recognisable British social chaos
* leave context unexplained
* end before resolution

This creates:

* curiosity
* comments
* "I know someone exactly like this"
* "what app is this 😭"

The roughness is part of the aesthetic:

* typing pauses
* awkward timing
* imperfect scrolling
* blurry photos
* badly framed screenshots

Later, the fictional world expands:

* fake TikToks inside the app
* Facebook fallout
* local gossip pages
* comments reacting to Tracey's behaviour

So the audience isn't just watching Tracey —
they're watching the internet react to Tracey.

## TikTok Concept: Arrested

**Chef's kiss.**

### Structure:

**0-3s: SMS thread opens**
- Tracey: "Babes, I'm in big TROUBLE!!"
- You: "What is it this time?"
- Tracey: "Ive only just been arrested."
- You: "Oh, crap here we go again"

**3-8s: Tracey's explanation (text or voice note clip)**
- "i was putting 12 bottles of prosecco, 3 cheese boards and that cream dave uses through the self-scan"
- "this guard appeared, jobsworth five foot nothing"
- "i turned round and knocked him out with my bag"

**8-15s: Police station photo**
- Tracey in the holding area, yellow vest, purple trackies, hoops, officer in background
- Long enough for the viewer to register it

**15-18s: WANTED poster effect**
- Photo gets hit with a big red WANTED stamp
- Maybe add "CHARGED WITH: Being Tracey" or just leave it blank and let the joke land

**18-20s: End card or CTA**
- "Download TUT!" or just the app icon

### Why this works:

It's comedic — she's not actually wanted, she's just in the holding area

It's instantly shareable (people will screenshot and send it)

It's thematically right for the "leaked memo" vibe

It makes the clip feel like a trailer for something bigger

This hits 20 seconds, hooks them, and sends them to the app store.
